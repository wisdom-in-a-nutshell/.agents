# Adi Writing

## Workflow

1. Read `references/voice.md`. It is the single source of truth for Adi's voice,
   clarity rules, formatting, and final editorial check.
2. Identify what the reader needs to understand, decide, feel, or do.
3. Inspect a small representative sample of Adi's current writing when useful.
4. Draft or edit for the requested format. Preserve the real point, factual
   nuance, and human texture.
5. Finish when the point has landed. Do not add generic polish or ceremony.

When Adi asks to shorten a title while keeping it "the same", preserve its
wording and structure as much as possible. Cut only what the limit requires.

## Delegation

Writing helpers may handle drafts, rewrites, tightening, and voice-sensitive
passes. Give them the target format, audience, source material, constraints, and
anything important to preserve or avoid. They should read this skill and inspect
relevant examples themselves.

Keep research, factual verification, and final editorial judgment with the
parent agent unless the task is explicitly pure writing.

## Iteration

Codify durable preferences and repeated corrections back into
`references/voice.md`. Do not overfit the skill to one draft or one format.
