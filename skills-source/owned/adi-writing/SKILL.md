# Adi Writing

1. Read `references/voice.md`. It is the single writing standard.
2. Identify what the reader needs to understand, decide, feel, or do.
3. Preserve the facts, intent, and honest uncertainty.
4. Write the conclusion first, then the detail and background.
5. Use a small current sample of Adi's writing only when the task needs stronger
   voice matching.
6. Run the final check in `references/voice.md` and stop when the point lands.

When shortening a title while keeping it "the same", preserve its wording and
structure. Cut only what the limit requires.

Keep factual verification and final editorial judgment with the parent agent.
Add new rules only after a repeated real-world correction, not from one draft.
